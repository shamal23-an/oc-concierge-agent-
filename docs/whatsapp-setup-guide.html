WhatsApp Business API Setup Guide

For: The Oyster Collection
Prepared by: AdeptNova
Date: 3 March 2026

Overview

This guide walks through the steps required to connect The Oyster Collection's WhatsApp number to the AI Concierge system via the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. Once complete, guests will be able to message the business WhatsApp number and receive instant, intelligent responses about properties, restaurants, activities, rates, and more.

Estimated time: 1-2 hours of setup + 2-5 business days for Meta business verification

What You'll Need

Before starting, please have the following ready:

  • Access to a computer (not mobile) with a web browser
  • The WhatsApp phone number: 066 190 xxxx
  • A Facebook account that can be used for business purposes
  • One of these business verification documents:
    • Business registration certificate / CIPC document
    • Utility bill showing the registered business name and address
    • Bank statement showing the registered business name
  • The Oyster Collection website URL: www.oystercollection.co.za

1 Create or Access Meta Business Manager

Meta Business Manager is the central hub where your WhatsApp API access is managed.

  1. Go to https://business.facebook.com
  2. If you already have an account — log in and proceed to Step 2
  3. If you don't have one:
    • Click "Create Account"
    • Enter business name: The Oyster Collection
    • Enter your name and business email
    • Fill in business details (address, website, phone)
    • Click "Submit"
Important: Use a real business email (e.g., info@oystercollection.co.za), not a personal email. This email will receive verification and notification emails from Meta.

2 Verify Your Business

Meta requires business verification before granting full WhatsApp API access. This is a one-time process and is the most important step — without it, you cannot send messages via the API.

  1. In Business Manager, go to Settings (gear icon, bottom left)
  2. Navigate to Security Centre
  3. Click "Start Verification"
  4. Choose your country: South Africa
  5. Enter your official business details:
    • Legal business name (must match exactly as per CIPC registration)
    • Registered address
    • Business phone number
    • Website: www.oystercollection.co.za
  6. Upload one verification document:
    • Option A: CIPC registration certificate
    • Option B: Recent utility bill with business name and address
    • Option C: Bank statement with business name and address
  7. Click "Submit"

Timeline: Meta typically reviews within 2-5 business days. You'll receive an email notification when approved.

If verification is rejected: The most common reason is a mismatch between the business name on the document and the name you entered. Ensure they match exactly (including "Pty Ltd" or similar). Resubmit with a different document if needed.

3 Create a Meta Developer App

In addition to Business Manager, you need a Meta Developer App — this is where WhatsApp Cloud API is enabled.

  1. Go to https://developers.facebook.com
  2. Log in with the same Facebook account used for Business Manager
  3. Click "My Apps" > "Create App"
  4. Select app type: "Business"
  5. App name: "Oyster Collection WhatsApp" (or similar)
  6. Connect it to your Meta Business Manager account
  7. Once created, find "WhatsApp" in the product list and click "Set Up"
  8. Accept the WhatsApp Cloud API terms and conditions
Note: This step creates the "App" container that holds your WhatsApp API configuration, webhook settings, and credentials.

4 Create a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA)

During the WhatsApp product setup (Step 3), you'll be prompted to create a WABA or link an existing one:

  1. In the WhatsApp setup flow, click "Create a WhatsApp Business Account"
  2. Business name: The Oyster Collection
  3. This WABA will hold your phone number(s) and messaging configuration

5 Register the Phone Number

Before this step: The phone number (066 190 xxxx) must NOT be currently registered on the regular WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app. If it is, it must be deregistered first.

If the number is currently on WhatsApp:

  1. Back up any important conversations on the phone
  2. Open WhatsApp on the phone with that number
  3. Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account
  4. Wait at least 5 minutes before proceeding

To register the number on WhatsApp Cloud API:

  1. In your Meta Developer App, navigate to WhatsApp > API Setup
  2. Scroll down to "Add Phone Number"
  3. Enter your business display name: The Oyster Collection
  4. Enter: +27 66 190 xxxx (South Africa country code +27)
  5. Choose verification method: SMS or Voice Call
  6. Enter the OTP verification code received on the phone
  7. Set a Two-Step Verification PIN (6-digit PIN — save this securely, it's required for future re-registrations)
Note: Meta reviews display names for policy compliance. Use your actual business name. Promotional text, all-caps, or misleading names will be rejected.

6 Create a System User and Generate Permanent Token

This step generates the API credentials the AI Concierge system needs. We can do this together on a call, or you can grant AdeptNova admin access to complete it.

Important: Meta provides a temporary test token during setup, but it expires in 24 hours. You must create a System User with a permanent token for production use.
  1. In Business Manager, go to Settings > Users > System Users
  2. Click "Add" to create a new system user
  3. Name: concierge-api
  4. Role: Admin
  5. Click "Add Assets":
    • Select Apps > your WhatsApp app
    • Grant Full Control
    • Select WhatsApp Accounts > your WABA
    • Grant Full Control
  6. Click "Generate New Token"
  7. Select the app you created in Step 3
  8. Enable the following permissions:
    • whatsapp_business_management
    • whatsapp_business_messaging
  9. Click "Generate Token"
  10. Copy the token immediately — it will not be shown again
Security: This token is equivalent to a password. Do not share it via email. We will provide a secure method for credential exchange (e.g., a one-time-view link or encrypted channel).

7 Enable Payment Method (Required for Production)

Meta requires a valid payment method on file before allowing production message sending:

  1. In Business Manager, go to Settings > Payments (or Billing)
  2. Add a credit card or payment method
  3. This covers any messaging costs beyond the free tier (first 1,000 service conversations/month are free)

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Will we lose our existing WhatsApp conversations?
If the number is currently on the WhatsApp app, existing chat history will be lost when migrating to the Cloud API. We recommend backing up important conversations before deregistering.
Q: Can we still use WhatsApp manually on a phone?
No — once a number is registered on the Cloud API, it cannot be used simultaneously on the WhatsApp mobile app. All messages are handled through the API. Complex queries that the AI cannot handle are escalated to your team.
Q: Is there a cost for WhatsApp messages?
Meta charges per conversation (24-hour window), not per individual message. The first 1,000 service conversations per month are free. After that, South African rates are approximately $0.03-0.08 per conversation. As of July 2025, Meta also applies per-message pricing for certain message categories.
Q: What if the AI can't answer a question?
The system gracefully escalates to a human team member. Guests receive a message like: "Let me connect you with our team for this. Someone will be in touch shortly."
Q: How long does the full setup take?
The technical setup takes 1-2 hours. The bottleneck is Meta's business verification, which takes 2-5 business days. Once credentials are shared with us, we go live within 24-48 hours.
